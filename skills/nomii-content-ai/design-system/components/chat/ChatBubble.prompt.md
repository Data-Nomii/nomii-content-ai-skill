One-line: White rounded bubble for an internal thought or open question the reader is asking themselves.

```jsx
<ChatBubble>¿Qué hago entonces?</ChatBubble>
<ChatBubble tail>¿O asumo que nunca me podré especializar?</ChatBubble>
```

Props: `tail` adds a speech tail; `align` left|center. Stack 2–3 vertically on a celeste flat slide to build the "y probablemente te preguntas" moment. Light weight text.
