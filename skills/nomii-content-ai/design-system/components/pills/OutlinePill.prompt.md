One-line: Thin-outline capsule used to split a feature or benefit into 2–4 scannable chips.

```jsx
<OutlinePill>Top 3 en innovación en salud</OutlinePill>
<OutlinePill tone="azul">Alta demanda de médicos</OutlinePill>
```

Variants: `tone` celeste | azul. `size` sm | md | lg. Non-italic Medium label. Stack several with wrap + gap to build the "desglose de características" clusters seen in the key visuals.
