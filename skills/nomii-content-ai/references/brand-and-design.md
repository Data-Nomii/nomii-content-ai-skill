# Brand And Design Rules

NOMII visual output must feel professional, human, clear, and strategic. The design system should prevent generic AI visuals by locking brand, typography, layout, and image use.

## Color

Core colors:

- Celeste: `#77BFD0`.
- Azul: `#104574`.
- Dark text: `#3C3C3B`.
- Soft background: `#F8F8F8`.

Avoid pure white as the main background unless matching an existing approved template. Use the soft background instead.

## Typography

Use Rubik only.

- Body: Rubik Light.
- Titles/keywords: Rubik Medium.
- Emphasis/pills: Rubik Medium Italic.

Do not use bold. Do not mix decorative fonts. Keep sentence case.

## Logo And Isotype

Use official assets only:

- blue logo;
- white logo;
- celeste isotype;
- white isotype.

Do not redraw, recolor, trace, distort, or approximate the marks. Do not invent logos inside generated images.

## Voice

Language: Spanish.

Tone:

- empathetic first;
- strategic second;
- clear and direct;
- professional but not cold;
- second person `tu`.

Avoid:

- emoji;
- overdramatic fear language;
- generic motivational copy;
- jargon-heavy migration copy;
- medical claims without source/context.

## Visual Style

Preferred imagery:

- medical or professional context;
- Germany, institutions, hospitals, universities, documents, or professional progress when relevant;
- clean composition;
- natural/cool light;
- human but not melodramatic;
- aspirational without looking like generic stock.

Avoid:

- text baked into photos;
- random graphics;
- excessive blur;
- dramatic filters;
- cluttered backgrounds behind copy;
- faces covered by text;
- low-resolution crops.

## Canvas Rules

- Carousel canvas: `1080x1440`.
- Safe area: `85px` top/bottom, `63px` sides.
- Logo on regular slides: top-right, around `200px` width when the template calls for it.
- CTA slide: centered isotype circle when applicable.

## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- colors match NOMII palette;
- typography is Rubik and no bold is used;
- no emoji appears in production copy;
- logo/isotype comes from official assets;
- text remains inside safe areas;
- text is legible at mobile size;
- image does not fight the text;
- faces, eyes, mouth, and upper torso are not covered when relevant;
- CTA is clear;
- the visual role matches the campaign objective.
